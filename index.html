Talk to us

Questions.

What scoring software do you work with?

Today we read LeagueLeader share reports. If you use another system or keep stats in a spreadsheet, tell us in the form below and we will look at connecting it.

Do players need an app or an account?

No. They open a link or scan a QR code. They can add the page to their Home Screen if they want it to feel like an app.

How fresh are the numbers?

The site refreshes about every hour by default. It can show new data only as fast as your scoring system publishes it.

Whose data is it?

Yours. We display the standings and stats your league already publishes, and we do not sell them.

Can it use our name, colors and web address?

Yes. Branding and a custom domain are part of setup.

What happens if a source stops working?

The site keeps showing the last good data, and we are alerted so the connection can be fixed.
